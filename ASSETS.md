## Sosyal paylaşım kartı

Yerel dosya: `public/og.png`. Yerleşik Imagegen aracı ile tek istekte üretildi; CLI kullanılmadı.

Üretim istemi:

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: landscape social preview card for a Turkish law firm website, approximately 1200×630 proportions.
Primary request: Create an elegant, restrained, premium editorial law brand image.
Scene/backdrop: Naturally sunlit travertine architectural arches on the right side, with ample clean warm ivory negative space on the left.
Style/medium: Sophisticated editorial architectural photography paired with beautifully legible corporate serif typography.
Composition/framing: Wide landscape card. Keep all text comfortably inside safe margins on the left half. Right half contains tactile travertine arches and soft natural architectural shadows, blending gracefully into the left ivory field.
Lighting/mood: Calm, grounded, warm natural daylight.
Color palette: Warm ivory #faf9f6, beige #eeeae2, oxblood burgundy #591620, charcoal.
Text (verbatim, Turkish spelling must be exact):
"ARLAS LEGAL"
"Hukuk & Danışmanlık"
"Sağlam bir temel. Güvenli bir gelecek."
Typography: Elegant corporate serif, crisp and readable. ARLAS LEGAL is the brand title in oxblood burgundy; the smaller secondary line immediately below is Hukuk & Danışmanlık. Headline below with generous spacing, may wrap into two balanced lines. Preserve every Turkish character including ı, ş, ğ, ü and noktalama.
Constraints: Only these three text strings. No people, no claims, no invented logo or monogram, no icons, no watermark, no additional text. Visually polished and understated.
```
